# Product

AisleMorrow: Keep retail shelves aligned with demand.

AI retail analytics software that analyzes shelf photographs and inventory records to identify stock gaps and recommend replenishment activities.

- Founder and CEO: Chinedu Iroegbu
- Co-founder and CTO: Mariam Bello
- Industry: Retail, Supply Chain Management, Computer Vision
- Customers: Supermarkets, retail chains and merchandising agencies
- Core features: Shelf image analysis, stock gap detection, replenishment forecasting, planogram comparison, store analytics
- MVP: Shelf photograph uploads and staff-reviewed replenishment alerts
- Planned AWS: SageMaker AI, S3, Lambda, DynamoDB, Bedrock
- Business model: Subscriptions per retail location
- Main action: Request a pilot
