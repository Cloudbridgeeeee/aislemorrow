How an AisleMorrow pilot runs

  1. Load the planograms and stock records

    We import your planograms and a daily inventory export for each pilot store.

  2. Photograph each shelf twice a day

    Staff walk each aisle with a phone; gaps appear in the list within minutes.

  3. Review and refill

    Supervisors confirm each alert, staff refill the shelf and the next photo round checks it.

AisleMorrow’s platform on AWS

The shelf platform we are building. The public demo calls none of these services.

  • S3 and Lambda

    Shelf photographs and stock exports, checked as they arrive

  • SageMaker AI

    Facing detection, gap scoring and replenishment forecasts

  • Bedrock

    Plain-language refill notes for each supervisor

  • DynamoDB

    Stores, aisles, alerts and the review log

Questions

What retail teams ask before a pilot.

Ask about a pilot

Which phones do store staff need?

An Android or iPhone with a camera. One aisle takes about a minute to photograph.

Does it order stock for us?

No. It drafts refill alerts; a supervisor confirms each one and your ordering stays as it is.

What does a store subscription cost?

A subscription per retail location per month. We quote after the pilot.

Do we need planograms?

They help with placement checks. Gap detection works without them.

Can merchandising agencies follow their stores?

Yes. Agencies can follow the stores they serve, with access each retailer approves.

How long are shelf photographs kept?

Ninety days by default. Each retailer can set a shorter period for its stores.
