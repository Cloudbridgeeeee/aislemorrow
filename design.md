Kind: software. Palette steel-orange (bg #eeeeeb, ink #1c1d1f, accent #c2501b). Type outfit. Hero collage, nav-centre, footer-dark, close-type, rhythm dark-band, buttons solid, mark dots.
Moments: word-highlight (statement), pinned-chapters (pilot steps).

Rules every change keeps:
- Kit system only (public/assets/system.css and layout.css); one accent; sentence case; one button pair.
- No captions under visuals, no invented proof, figures only from the brief; prices on request.
- Motion: the hero entrance, the two dealt moments, the hover and press layer. No scroll fades.
- Demo workspace motion follows Emil Kowalski: ease-out cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1), 150 to 250 ms, press at scale .97, reduced motion rests.
- Anti-slop checks (Impeccable, Unslop, frontend-design): no purple gradients, emoji labels, all-caps eyebrows or uneven spacing.
